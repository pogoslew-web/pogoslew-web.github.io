Overview

A marble hall west of the Awakened Plaza where awakened buy and sell gear with every server, in shards: auctions (the best bid wins) and buy-now prices, a price chart of each item and a price suggested from supply and demand. Up to 8 listings at a time for 2, 6, 12, 24 or 48 h; a bid raises the price by 5% at least, and a bid in the last minute extends the sale. Listing fee 1% of the price (5 to 25,000 shards), 4% taken on a sale; earnings wait in MY SALES until COLLECT ALL.
