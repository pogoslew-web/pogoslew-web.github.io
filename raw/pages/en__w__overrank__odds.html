Pity, modifiers and how to read

  • Luck bonuses are added, not multiplied: multiplier = 1 + the sum of the bonuses, x6 at most. It multiplies the chance of the lines marked with a sparkle, 100% at most.
  • In a guardian's spoils only the grades above the centre rank get the luck (x3 at most, with the dungeon stars bonus), then the table is scaled back to 100%.
  • "At least 1 in N tries" = 1 - (1 - p)^N: the chance to get it at least once.
  • "1 in N" is an average: you can get it sooner or much later.
